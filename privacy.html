Privacy Policy

This application is a personal rclone configuration used only by its owner.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, to upload and manage the owner's files.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data from any other person.
  • No data is sent to any third party. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
